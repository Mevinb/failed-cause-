## 2. Open Theoretical & Engineering Questions Under Investigation

1. **Optimal Allocation Between Parameters and Recurrent Iterations:** Under fixed inference FLOPs, when does a smaller weight-tied core iterated $K$ times outperform an $L = K \times L_{\text{core}}$ untied Transformer?
2. **Surprise Signal Geometry:** Does gating fast-weight writes by associative delta error $\|v_t - M_{t-1} k_t\|_2$ outperform purely learned input-linear gates $\sigma(w^\top x_t)$?
3. **Continual Plasticity vs. Consolidation Trade-off:** What combination of shrink-and-perturb / weight decay plus episodic replay maximizes the joint harmonic mean of Old-Task Retention and New-Task Final Accuracy?
4. **Latent Lookahead vs. Reactive Policy:** On environments with irreversible traps (Key-Door-Trap gridworlds), how many latent world-model rollout steps suffice to close the gap with oracle breadth-first search?
5. **Depth-Sharing vs. Depth-Index Conditioning (NEW — Gen-2 finding):** Is the ~150× causal-grok speedup from sinusoidal depth encoding due specifically to cross-depth gradient sharing (every step trains all K), or to the encoding merely being on-manifold at eval? `H3+sinusoidal` ablation (no slots) isolates it.
6. **Adaptive Halting:** The K=1→16 curve peaks at K=6 — can the `dz`-norm contraction signal or the verifier select per-token/per-episode stopping depth to spend compute only where it helps?
7. **Slot Write Selectivity:** Top-1-per-16-token-chunk writes miss facts (gap≤103 residual cost). Does per-head top-k, learned write allocation, or a mid-depth second bank close the last gap to the full-context baseline?
8. **Symbolic vs. Gradient Program Induction:** FiLM program vectors refined by gradient got cell-accuracy gains but 0 exact solves — is a discrete program-search layer (e.g. library-of-primitives enumeration guided by the reasoner's prior) required for compositional ARC?
